# Catalog of the Van Gestel video rides

Generated by `pnpm fetch:video-catalog`; do not edit by hand. `index.json` lists every route, `<id>/route.json` holds its profile, path, notes and segments, `<id>/thumbnail.jpg` a picture of the video.

The routes, their tracks, notes and pictures are by Fred Van Gestel, [cycling.vangestel.online](https://cycling.vangestel.online/indoor/videos/), licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). The profiles and paths are derived from his GPX tracks and shared under the same license. This folder is not covered by the AGPL of the plugin's code.
