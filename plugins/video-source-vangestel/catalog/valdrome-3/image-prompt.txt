A photograph of the Claps de Luc near Luc-en-Diois, Drôme, France, on a bright summer day. A country road runs down the Drôme valley past an enormous chaos of giant limestone blocks, the remains of a mountainside that collapsed into the valley centuries ago and dammed the river. Huge pale boulders, some the size of houses, lie scattered across the slope and along the river; the turquoise Drôme flows in a narrow channel between them. Steep forested mountains and a bare broken cliff rise behind under a blue sky with scattered clouds. Clear light, a sense of geological drama in a quiet valley; 24 mm lens, f/8.
