# ADR 0068: App State and Services Beside the Simulation Bus

Status: **accepted** on 2026-11-07 by the maintainer, with the implementation brief `ridrs_elephant/prompts/14-app-state-and-services.md` and the proposed AGENTS.md rule 13 (added in stage 4). Stages 0–4 are built (2026-11-07); recording, devices and the BLE bridge follow in a second brief. This record grows with the stages: each one adds what it built under "Implementation".

## Context

The event bus carries the simulation (inputs, derived events; ADRs 0039, 0055) and, since the training plan, the recovery offer and the video downloads, also app state and app commands. Everything a UI client publishes is an input; the recorder writes every event of a ride and its 60-s pre-roll; continuing an interrupted ride (ADR 0057) starts every plugin and re-executes every recorded input. The full analysis, the references (Home Assistant, ROS 2, VS Code, Grafana, BFF, microkernel, deterministic simulation, CQRS) and the alternatives are in [app-state-and-services.md](../ideas/app-state-and-services.md).

### Evidence (stage 0, 2026-11-07)

`tests/features/recording/continue-without-app-commands.feature` (tagged `@wip`, left out by both Cucumber profiles until stage 3) fails today exactly as the proposal predicted:

| Scenario | Failure |
|---|---|
| A download asked for during the ride is not asked for again when the ride continues | `"test-app" received "route.fetch-requested" 2 times: once in the ride, again when it continued` |
| An upload asked for just before the ride is not sent again when the ride continues | `"test-app" received "training.upload-requested" 2 times: once in the ride, again when it continued` (the upload lies in the pre-roll) |
| The recording of a ride holds no app state | `The recording holds 10 "route.offers-changed" events` |

With the real plugins this means: `video-source-vangestel` starts the download again, `planner-intervals-icu` uploads the activity a second time (it does not check), and every percent of a download during a ride is a line in the journal. The same holds for every other app command on the bus (`training.connect-requested`, `recording.delete-requested`, `device.*-requested`, `bridge.*-requested`) when it falls into a ride's journal.

## Decision

1. **Which channel:** "Does a replay or a continued ride need it to reproduce the ride?" Yes → the simulation bus. No → app state (a value) or a service (a request). Both → the owner hands it to the ride as an input (as the garage does, ADR 0060).
2. **App state** beside the bus: topics with string keys, one owner per key, validated on write, delivered as a snapshot and then the changed keys (coalesced per microtask), in memory, never recorded.
3. **Services** beside the bus: request and response validated, one provider or routed to the owner of a key; quick answers; long work is a call, state for its progress and a cancel call.
4. **Only input-side plugin types** (`transport`, `storage`, `ui`, `device`; the list of ADR 0054) own, watch, provide or call; every use is declared in the manifest; UI clients get `ctx.state.watch` and `ctx.call` with allow-lists in `ui.state` and `ui.calls`.
5. **Order:** stage 0 (done), stage 1 (the channels), stage 2 (route offers), stage 3 (training), stage 4 (docs, AGENTS.md rule 13, a contract test against new app events on the bus). Recording, devices and the BLE bridge follow in a second brief.

Rejected: a `journal: false` flag on app events (fixes the journal, adds no request/response and no state per key); finer route events (journal unchanged); a separate BFF layer (one process: a state-owning host plugin is its UI's backend); a client-only store (host side and journal unchanged). Reasons in the proposal, section 7.

## Consequences

- Until stage 3 the problem stayed; the `@wip` scenarios named it (since stage 3 they pass). Until the second brief, recording, device and bridge commands are still re-executed when they fall into a journal.
- The `@wip` tag is new: `cucumber.mjs` leaves it out of both profiles. It is for scenarios that show a known, accepted problem, and each carries the stage that removes the tag (none since stage 3).
- `route-catalog`, the `route.offers` capability and the `route.*` and `training.*` events go away in stages 2 and 3.

## Implementation

(Continued for recording, devices and the BLE bridge in [ADR 0069](0069-recording-devices-and-bridge-beside-the-bus.md).)

- **Stage 0 (2026-11-07):** `tests/features/recording/continue-without-app-commands.feature`, `tests/steps/app-commands.steps.ts` (probe `test-app` counting events; publishing through the UI bridge with a payload; the open recording holds no event of a type), `@wip` in `cucumber.mjs`.
- **Stage 1 (2026-11-07): the two channels.** Contracts (`@ridrs/contracts`, Apache-2.0): `app-state.ts` (`defineState`, `StateView`, `PluginStateContext`), `services.ts` (`defineService`, which checks `key: z.string()` for keyed services; `ServiceCaller`, `ServiceHandler`, `PluginServicesContext`); `PluginContext.state` and `.services`; manifest fields `state: { owns, watches }` and `services: { provides, calls }` (input-side types only, `invalid-manifest` otherwise) and `ui.state`, `ui.calls` (ui plugins only); `UiRegistry.state`, `.calls` and per plugin; new codes (diagnostics `unknown-state`, `unknown-service`, `ambiguous-service`, `state-feedback`; errors `state-key-owned`, `undeclared-state`, `invalid-state-value`, `undeclared-service`, `service-unavailable`, `unknown-key`, `invalid-request`, `invalid-response`, `service-failed`, `state-not-allowed`, `call-not-allowed`). Core: `state-store.ts` (ownership, validation once per write, snapshot then changed keys in one `queueMicrotask` flush per tick, writes during a delivery in the next flush, `state-feedback`, removal on stop), `service-registry.ts` (one provider or the key's owner, request and response validation, handler after a microtask, `service-failed` wrapping), resolution (`unknown-state`, `unknown-service`, `ambiguous-service` block), `PluginHost` (`PluginHostOptions.states`/`.services`, `ctx.state`/`ctx.services` with `permission-denied` by type and `undeclared-*` by manifest, `getState`, `callService`, `observeState`, `watchState`). UI: `UiBridge.watchState` (`StateUpdate` messages: snapshot, then values and removed keys) and `UiBridge.call`; the in-process bridge enforces the registry's `state`/`calls` and ends watches on `dispose`; `UiContext.state.watch` and `UiContext.call`. No concrete topic or service is registered yet. Tests: `tests/features/core/app-state.feature` (10 scenarios, one under `@fake-timers`, one with a recorded ride), `tests/features/core/services.feature` (11, one continuing a ride), `tests/features/ui/app-state-in-the-ui.feature` (4, fixture `tests/fixtures/plugins/app-state-ui`), steps `tests/steps/app-state.steps.ts`, World support (`stateOf`, test topics `test.offer`, `test.other`, services `test.fetch`, `test.ping`); Vitest `state-store.test.ts` (with 1000 offer-sized writes under 50 ms), `service-registry.test.ts`, `in-process-bridge.test.ts`, manifest tests, `tests/unit/app-channels.test.ts` (no simulation manifest declares state or services). Docs: [app-state-and-services.md](../architecture/app-state-and-services.md), core.md, ui-and-activity.md.
- **Stage 2 (2026-11-07): route offers.** `route.ts`: `routeOfferState` (`route.offer`, keyed by the offer id), `routeFetchService` and `routeRemoveService` (`{ key }` → `{}`, keyed by `route.offer`); `RouteOffers`, `routeOffersCapability` and the events `route.catalog-changed`, `route.offers-changed`, `route.fetch-requested`, `route.remove-requested` are removed, with their registrations (`apps/native/src/compose.ts`, `apps/headless/src/main.ts`, `tests/support/world.ts`, which register `route.offer`, `route.fetch` and `route.remove` instead) and samples. `plugins/route-catalog` is deleted (validation is the store's, duplicates are ownership). `ui-landscape-demo` sets its demo course at start; `video-source-vangestel` sets every offer at start, changes one key per change of a route (`VideoDownloads` calls back with the route's id), provides `route.fetch` (rejects with the reason when a download cannot start: `VideoDownloads.refusal`; otherwise answers at once and downloads; `ready` or `fetching` stays as it is) and `route.remove` (answers once the folder is gone). `ui-area-ride` watches `route.offer` (by provider, then title: `orderOffers`), calls the services and shows a refusal in the details' status. `ui-world-video` watches `route.offer` and loads its route again only when that route's key changes; the `filesKey` workaround is gone. The host now refuses `ctx.state.set`/`remove` of a plugin that is not started (`plugin-not-active`), so late work of a stopped plugin cannot bring its keys back. `recording.test.ts` picks events by their export name (`…Event`): topics have an id and a schema too. Scenarios: "A download that cannot start says why" (`route-offers.feature`), "Progress changes only the downloading route" (`video-downloads.feature`); `route-catalog` left the plugin lists of four features, every `Then` unchanged. Stage 0: the download scenario calls `route.fetch` through the UI bridge (`Then` unchanged); the app state scenario writes `route.offer` ten times and also checks that the recording holds nothing of the topic. Both pass; the upload scenario still fails until stage 3.
- **Stage 3 (2026-11-07): training.** `training.ts`: topics `training.account` and `training.plan` (key: the service, `intervals.icu`) and `training.upload` (key: the session id), services `training.connect`, `training.disconnect`, `training.refresh` (`{ key }`) and `training.upload` (`{ key, sessionId, file }`), all keyed by `training.account`; the seven `training.*` events are removed with their registrations and samples. `planner-intervals-icu` owns the three topics and provides the four services: it sets the account and the plan where it accepted inputs before (at start: disconnected, or connected with the plan, or the cached plan offline), answers once the work started (a connect during other work is dropped, as before) and still loads the plan after `recording.completed` (an event until the second brief). `ui-training-plan` watches the account and the plan and calls connect, disconnect and refresh; `ui-debriefing` watches the account and the uploads, calls `training.upload` and `training.connect`, and shows a refused upload's reason with Try again (its `recording.*` events stay). `PluginHost.observeCalls` (`ServiceRegistry.observe`) taps every finished call with its answer or error; the secrecy step "no event on the bus contains the intervals.icu token" now also checks every app state write and every service call. The stage-0 feature lost `@wip`: the upload is a call of `training.upload` through the UI bridge, the probe owns an account key and answers both services; all three scenarios pass (`Then` unchanged). Every `Then` of `connect-intervals-icu.feature`, `next-workout.feature` and `debriefing.feature` is unchanged.
- **Stage 4 (2026-11-07): finish.** AGENTS.md rule 13 ("Simulation on the bus, app state beside it"), approved with the brief; it follows rule 12, the brief's "after rule 11" predates rule 12. `packages/contracts/src/app-events.test.ts` lists the simulation's requests and changes and the app events still on the bus (the table of [recording-and-replay.md](../architecture/recording-and-replay.md), "What the journal holds"); it fails for a new `*-requested` or `*-changed` event on neither list and for any `route.*` or `training.*` event. The app list must shrink to empty in the second brief. Docs: recording-and-replay.md ("What the journal holds"), app-state-and-services.md (rule 13, topics and services today), the proposal's status.
