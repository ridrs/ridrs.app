# ADR 0059: The App's Areas as a Clickable Preview; User Settings for UI Clients

Status: accepted on 2026-11-06 by the maintainer and implemented the same day. Takes up the menu structure of [main-menu-structure.md](../ideas/main-menu-structure.md) as a clickable preview of the early alpha. Extends [ADR 0054](0054-plugin-settings-and-remembered-devices.md) (the `user` scope, settings for UI clients) and [ADR 0058](0058-main-screen-layout-by-configuration.md) (global actions, the areas in the content area). Amended by [ADR 0060](0060-equipment-garage-and-the-frozen-bicycle.md): the garage (`ui-garage`, no preview: it changes the ride) is a sixth menu entry after Me; Me keeps the achievements. Amended by [ADR 0071](0071-global-screens-in-a-dialog-over-the-page.md): Me and the garage leave the menu for the top bar, and the global screens open in a dialog over the page.

## Context

The maintainer wants a clickable dummy application that gives a feel for the final app: the five areas of the idea document (Today, Ride, Training, Events, Me) and its global elements (Search, the device status, Settings, the profile), each area a plugin of its own, and every change taking effect like in a phone app, without a Save. Placeholders are acceptable in the early alpha. The maintainer chose (2026-11-06): changes are kept **persistently** (not only in memory), the global elements sit **in the top bar**, and placeholder content carries a small **"Preview"** label.

UI client code could not keep anything: `ctx.settings` ([ADR 0054](0054-plugin-settings-and-remembered-devices.md)) exists only on a plugin's host side, and only for machine configuration.

## Decision

- **Five areas, each a plugin** (AGPL-3.0-or-later): Today is the main screen (its menu entry, formerly Home, is now "Today"); `ui-area-ride` (screen `ride`), `ui-area-training` (`training`), `ui-area-events` (`events`) and `ui-area-me` (`me`) each contribute a screen and a `menu/item` (order 10–40). The main screen's layout names their screens in `screens`, so they open beside the menu ([ADR 0058](0058-main-screen-layout-by-configuration.md)). No area imports another or knows the main screen; Back leaves an area for Today.
- **Global actions** in the top bar: the main screen offers the extension point `main/action`; `layout.json` names the actions in order (`actions`): `ui-search/search`, `ui-devices/status`, `ui-settings/settings`, `ui-area-me/profile`. The head row holds the top bar (on Today only) and the actions; the actions stay while an area is shown, and at the compact width, where the top bar is hidden, they stay as a slim row at the top. An action is one `button[data-action]` with an `aria-label`, an icon `[data-action-icon]` (its mask in `--icon`) and optionally a dot `[data-action-tone]` (`ok`, `busy`, `warn`); the main screen styles them all. The device status replaces the menu entry Devices of `ui-devices`; Search and Settings are plugins of their own (`ui-search`, `ui-settings`, screens `search` and `settings`).
- **Cards on Today** come from the areas: `ui-area-ride/ride-again`, `ui-area-events/next-event`, `ui-area-me/weekly-goal`, placed in `layout.json`. Each can be hidden on Today ("Hide …") and shown again in its area ("Show … on Today").
- **User settings for UI clients.** A new persistence scope `user` (manifest `"persistence": ["user"]`, `ctx.settings.user(document)`): the rider's preferences and choices, documents at `config/user/<plugin id>/<name>.json`, otherwise as `machine` (versioned, validated, written atomically, in order). `UiContext.settings` gives a plugin's client code the same settings access as its host side, through the bridge (`UiBridge.settings(pluginId)`, `PluginHost.getClientSettings(pluginId)`): one access per plugin and host, so writes from both sides keep one order. The permission is the manifest's, as on the host side. The domain is "user profile and preferences" of [ADR 0052](0052-persistence-domains-and-plan.md); the athlete profile (P7) moves the documents into SQLite behind the same access.
- **No Save.** Every change is written when it is made. `@ridrs/ui-preview` (a package, AGPL) holds what the areas share: `userDocument(document, initial)`, a value shared by all components of a plugin's client module and kept per settings access of the host (read once, `initial` until then); the "Preview" label (`[data-preview]`); the area page, the Today card, the menu entry and the action button; the look of the preview (`PREVIEW_CSS`, on the tokens of ADR 0058). Only UI plugin clients import it (dependency-cruiser).
- **What works and what is a placeholder.** Real: navigation, Go and "Ride … again" (they start the ride, on the demo landscape whatever the route), the device status, and keeping every change. Placeholders, marked "Preview": routes and videos, workouts, plans, events, achievements, the search's own catalogue (the areas do not offer their content to other plugins yet), the settings (nothing reads units, language, sounds or smoothing), FTP, weight and the weekly goal (no simulation value: they would have to enter a ride as inputs, ADR 0052), and the hours ridden this week.
- **The areas in brief.** Ride: filters (flat, hilly, under 45 min, favourites), worlds and routes, real video, a favourite star per route, a route's details with Go (two clicks from the list to the ride). Training: this week's calendar (select a day; add a rest day, a note, an outdoor ride; move an entry a day later; remove it; Undo, up to ten changes, kept across restarts), workouts with favourites and "Schedule … on <day>", plans and a suggestion as placeholders. Events: races, group rides, challenges, clubs with Join. Me: FTP, weight and the weekly goal in steps, activities, achievements and the account as placeholders. The garage is its own area (ADR 0060), which is real. Search: one field over routes, workouts and events, kinds as filters, recent searches. Settings: basics (units, language, sounds) and Advanced (power smoothing, frame rate), Reset to defaults.

## Consequences

- `@ridrs/contracts`: the scope `user` (`persistenceScopeSchema`, `PluginSettingsContext.user`). `@ridrs/ui-sdk`: `UiContext.settings`, `UiBridge.settings`. `@ridrs/core`: `PluginHost.getClientSettings`. A remote bridge would have to carry settings reads and writes.
- The menu now offers Today, Ride, Training, Events and Me; Devices moved to the actions. Tests: `app-areas.feature`, `app-area-changes.feature`, `main-menu.feature` (with Ride as the contributed entry), unit tests of the calendar, the route filters, the search and the `user` scope.
- Lucide icons copied unchanged are excluded from Biome (`**/assets/icons`): they must not be edited (ADR 0047).
- Out of this preview: "Schedule" from Ride and Events into the calendar (it needs a contract between areas), sorting and further search filters, calendar export, a map, pacers, settings that take effect, the athlete profile.
