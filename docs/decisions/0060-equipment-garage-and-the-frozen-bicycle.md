# ADR 0060: The Equipment Garage and the Bicycle Frozen for a Ride

Status: accepted on 2026-11-06 by the maintainer (brief 12, revision 2; "build all three" shifting modes) and implemented the same day. Amends [ADR 0036](0036-config-snapshot-capability.md) (frozen values in the sections), [ADR 0055](0055-virtual-gears-and-never-stale-series.md) (the gears per ride) and [ADR 0059](0059-app-areas-as-a-clickable-preview.md) (the garage is a top-level area of its own, apart from Me's achievements, and is no preview).

## Context

The idea page ([main-menu-structure.md](../ideas/main-menu-structure.md), "an equipment garage") proposes a garage of the equipment one rides with, because it feeds the physics. `physics-bicycle` had a fixed 10 kg, `control-virtual-gears` one fixed table of 24 geometric gears (ADR 0055). The maintainer decided that the bicycle's weight and drivetrain take effect, are frozen when a ride starts, are recorded with it, and that a replay runs with the recorded values, not the current settings. The drivetrain is the real one: Shimano Di2 2 × 12 with 50-34 chainrings and an 11-34 cassette, shifted like the physical Di2 in the mode the rider sets.

## Decision

- **The bicycle enters a ride as an input.** `equipment.bicycle-changed` (`@ridrs/contracts`, `bicycleSchema`): name, mass and the drivetrain resolved from its specification (`id`, chainring and sprocket teeth, `shifting: { mode, semiSynchroCompensation, fullSynchro: { toLarge, toSmall } }`, `startGear`). It carries the teeth and the shift map themselves, so a later change of a specification file never changes a recorded ride. The garage accepts it at host start and again when a ride finishes; its client publishes it after every change. Changing the bicycle never changes a ride under way.
- **`activity-session` freezes it.** It keeps the latest bicycle; when it starts a new session it publishes the lifecycle event `activity.equipment` `{ sessionId, bicycle }` in the same turn, right before `activity.state-changed` `running`. Pause, resume and a continued ride stay in their session and freeze nothing again. Without a bicycle it publishes nothing.
- **Consumers.** `physics-bicycle` subscribes to `activity.equipment` (a lifecycle event: AGENTS.md rule 11 and its test hold) and returns the frozen mass with the new origin `'equipment'`; the rolling resistance stays the default. `control-virtual-gears` takes the frozen drivetrain for its session. Without `activity.equipment` both keep their defaults (10 kg; the 24 virtual gears of ADR 0055, any shift button shifting one gear), so rides without a garage are unchanged.
- **Shifting like the Di2** (`plugins/control-virtual-gears/drivetrain.ts`, a pure function of drivetrain, gear, shifter and direction). A gear is a chainring and a sprocket, numbered as Shimano does (chainring 1 the smallest, sprocket 1 the largest); its ratio, chainring ÷ sprocket, is the temporal series as before. The controller's left buttons are the left shifter, the right ones the right shifter; "up" is harder.
  - **Manual:** the left shifter moves the front derailleur, the right one the rear; nothing moves beyond the ends.
  - **Semi-synchro:** a front shift moves the rear by the compensation (2 sprockets) the other way.
  - **Full synchro:** the right shifter alone runs through the range: shifting harder on the small chainring from sprocket 7 or beyond switches to the large one and sprocket 5; shifting easier on the large chainring from sprocket 2 or below switches to the small one and sprocket 4. On 50-34 / 11-34: 34 × 17 → 50 × 21 and 50 × 30 → 34 × 24. The left shifter still shifts the front, compensated as in semi-synchro.
  - A press shifts once; holding shifts nothing more. The activity screen names the derailleur (`drivetrain.shift-requested.derailleur`, the rear without one); `drivetrain.gear-changed` carries the `chainring` and `sprocket` teeth and the `front` and `rear` positions with a drivetrain, `index` and `count` being the gear's rank among all 24 distinct ratios (what the trainer load counts). The capability's `ratios` are the session's (a getter).
- **The specification file** `plugins/ui-garage/equipment/shimano-di2-2x12-50-34-11-34.json`: chainrings 34, 50; cassette 34, 30, 27, 24, 21, 19, 17, 15, 14, 13, 12, 11 (CS-R8100-12 / CS-R7101); semi-synchro compensation 2; the full-synchro shift points above; no blocked combinations; with the evidence and sources of each value. Shimano's own documents could not be read (403): the cassette teeth are confirmed by several retailers, the compensation of 2 by several independent sources; the shift points are Shimano's 11-speed default (dealer's manual DM-RARD010-03, measured on 50-34 / 11-28 by a German test: 2.27 → 2.63, 2.0 → 1.62), applied to 12-speed by gear position, **not confirmed for 12-speed**. The maintainer may check them in E-TUBE PROJECT; the file is the one place to change.
- **The garage** is the plugin `ui-garage` (AGPL-3.0-or-later, type `ui`, `"persistence": ["user"]`). It owns the document `bicycle` (version 1, scope `user`: the rider's equipment, into the athlete profile with P7): `name` (1–40 characters), `mass` (5–25 kg), `drivetrain` (a specification id), `shiftMode` (`manual` | `semi-synchro` | `full-synchro`, default `manual`), `startGear` (default 34 × 17). **A top-level menu entry "Garage"** with Lucide's `wrench` icon (ADR 0047; `menu/item`, order 50, after Me; decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-06 instead of the brief's place under Me). Its screen `garage` opens beside the menu (`layout.json` `screens`): name, weight (± 0.5 kg), the drivetrain's teeth, the shifting mode, the start chainring and start sprocket; every change is kept at once and handed to the next ride; while a ride is under way it says that changes apply from the next ride. Back returns to Today. No "Preview" label: it works.
- **Me** keeps **Achievements** (still a preview) and no longer mixes the garage into them. **Today's start card** names the bicycle ("Riding My bike · 10.0 kg", not focusable). The gear widget shows the gear large as its positions, front × rear ("1×1" to "2×12", without "of 24") and "50 × 17" below as a smaller second line; the chainring and the sprocket are each focusable, ↑ ↓ shift the front on the chainring and the rear on the sprocket; + and − shift the rear.
- **Recording and replay.** The pre-roll keeps the latest `equipment.bicycle-changed` (as it keeps the latest connection state per device), because the bicycle is usually handed over long before a ride. `activity.equipment` is derived and compared. The `config.snapshot` sections of `physics-bicycle` and `control-virtual-gears` report the frozen values (taken at `running`). A replay leaves the garage out (type `ui`) and injects its recorded input: the ride replays with the recorded bicycle whatever the garage holds now. A recording without the input (from before the garage, or without it) replays with the defaults; `activity.equipment` is then not in the recording and not compared. **Continuing** (ADR 0057) re-enters the recorded bicycle from the journal before the start request, so the continued ride keeps its frozen bicycle; the garage hands over its current bicycle again when that ride finishes.

## Consequences

- New: `equipment.bicycle-changed`, `activity.equipment`, the origin `'equipment'`, `drivetrain.shift-requested.derailleur`, `drivetrain.gear-changed.chainring/sprocket`; plugin `ui-garage` with its specification files. The composition roots and the test world know the two events.
- Tests: `equipment/bicycle.feature` (weight changes the climb: 14.90 km/h with 10 kg, 13.82 km/h with 18 kg at 200 W on 5 %; the three modes; the activity screen's derailleurs; a change during a ride; the replay after a change; a recording without a bicycle; a continued ride and the next one), `ui/garage.feature`, unit tests of the shift logic.
- The rider's weight in Me stays a placeholder (it would take the same path).
- **Out of scope:** several bicycles and switching between them, tyres (rolling resistance), riding position (drag), wear in kilometres per part, the trainer and sensors linked to a bicycle, a picture, editing the synchronized shift map (E-TUBE), shifting several gears by holding a button (its defaults are not known), the time a derailleur takes to shift, cross-chaining wear, mechanical groupsets and other makes (SRAM AXS, Campagnolo) until their specification files are written.
