GEOS (libgeos, libgeos_c) - GNU LGPL 2.1. Dynamically linked by shapely. Source: https://libgeos.org/ . The library files sit
unmodified in the sidecar folder and can be replaced by another build of the same ABI.

libosmium / protozero / expat / zlib / lz4 (inside osmium) - Boost Software License / BSD-2 / MIT / zlib terms; see the osmium
licence text above and https://osmcode.org/libosmium/ .
Python (PSF License 2.0) and its bundled libraries (OpenSSL under Apache-2.0 where present, libffi, expat, zlib, xz, bzip2,
mpdecimal) - https://docs.python.org/3/license.html .
